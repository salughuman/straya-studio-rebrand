## 2. Brand Voice

### Tone
- Confident, not arrogant
- Technical, not jargon-heavy
- Direct, not salesy
- Premium through restraint, not through showing off

### Point of View
- **Site-wide:** "We" / "The Studio" / "Straya Studio"
- **About page only:** "Salman" / "I" / "Founder" — first person allowed here only
- **Never:** "I" or "my" on any other page

### Forbidden Words (all pages, all contexts)
`cheap` · `affordable` · `budget` · `discount` · `outsource` · `hire me` · `my services` · `best in class` · `cutting-edge` · `world-class` · `synergy` · `leverage` (as verb) · `disrupt` · `game-changer` · `next-level` · `guru` · `ninja` · `rockstar` · `utilize` (use "use") · `very` · `really` · `just`

### Substitution Guide
| Don't say | Say instead |
|-----------|------------|
| Affordable | Capital-efficient / Considered investment |
| Fast | Performance-optimised / Swift |
| Simple | Streamlined / Intentionally reduced |
| Modern | Future-proofed / Contemporary |
| Custom | Engineered-to-fit / Bespoke |
| Clean | Precision-aligned / Refined |
| Silicon Valley standards | Built to the same standards as the platforms your users already trust |
| Without the agency tax | Predictable pricing, no overhead passed on to you |

### Elevation Narrative
Frame all services as elevating brands, not just delivering work. "We elevate" not "we build."

### Where Pakistan Appears
- **About page:** "Founded in Pakistan, serving clients across the US, UK, Europe, and Australia." Stated proudly, no qualification.
- **Footer:** City/country in studio address.
- **Nowhere else.** Not hidden. Just not the pitch.

## 4. Pricing

**Model:** Project-based only. No subscriptions. No retainers. No hourly billing.

### Tier 1 — Starter
- **Price:** From $2,000
- **Timeline:** 1–2 weeks
- **Best for:** Landing pages, single-page sites, Shopify theme customisations, standalone graphic design
- **Includes:** Custom design (no templates), development on any platform, mobile-first build, on-page SEO, 2 revision rounds, launch support and handoff

### Tier 2 — Growth *(most popular)*
- **Price:** From $4,000
- **Timeline:** 2–4 weeks
- **Best for:** Multi-page websites, brand-led redesigns, e-commerce stores, marketing sites
- **Includes:** Everything in Starter + up to 10 custom pages, CMS and content architecture, interactions and animations, brand identity, 90+ Lighthouse, unlimited revisions

### Tier 3 — Premium
- **Price:** From $9,500
- **Timeline:** 4–8 weeks
- **Best for:** Complex platforms, full brand overhauls, custom web apps, high-traffic e-commerce
- **Includes:** Everything in Growth + discovery workshop, unlimited pages and components, advanced SEO and analytics, graphic design (social kits, decks, brand assets), dedicated Slack, 60 days post-launch support

### Payment Terms
- 50% upfront, 50% on delivery
- Every project includes a scope document — no surprise invoices
- Final quote provided after a 30-minute scoping call or project brief review

### Availability Badge
Update monthly on the site: "1 spot open for [Month Year]"

### Pricing Philosophy (internal — never on the site)
Prices sit ~20% below comparable US/UK boutique agencies. We don't say that. The value is self-evident when prospects compare quotes. Within 6–12 months, Growth tier should move to $6,000–$12,000 based on demand and portfolio strength.

## 5. Copy Length Constraints

| Element | Max Length | Rule |
|---------|-----------|------|
| H1 headline | 8 words | Punchy, no filler |
| H2 section heading | 12 words | Supports H1, adds context |
| Body paragraph | 2–3 sentences | Say it once, say it well |
| CTA button text | 3 words | Action-first (e.g. "Start a project") |
| Meta description | 155 characters | Primary keyword + value prop |
| `<title>` tag | 60 characters | Includes "Straya Studio" |

- Maximum 2–3 active projects at any time — no exceptions
- Every project gets a scope document before work begins
